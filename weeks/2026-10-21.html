What The Flint Focus is, and why it matters

The Flint Focus is where our announcements, updates, and dates live now. A new one goes up each week by Tuesday at 5:00 PM at the latest, and sometimes sooner. The PIN is in the email that comes with it.

Here's why. I don't want our team meetings to be me telling you to do this by that date. I'd rather we spend that time on things we do together: learning from each other, working at our tables, and having some fun.

Why it matters: when everyone shows up already knowing what's coming, our meetings can be shorter, more hands-on, and more about you. Read and complete it by 8:00 PM Wednesday. At the end you'll get a code to email me.

Step 3 of 8

Our meetings, our pace

How long a meeting takes is up to all of us. Each agenda has a pace built in. When we stay with it, we get through everything and get out sooner.

Several of you have told me the side conversations and running comments make meetings feel longer than they need to be. I hear that. This format is part of the fix. The rest is on all of us: respect each other's time and stay with the room.
