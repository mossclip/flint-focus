Step 7 of 13

What you're looking for

Substance use
Alcohol, high-risk drinking gear (beer funnels, beer pong tables), drugs or paraphernalia
Fire safety
Candles or incense, smoking, covered smoke detectors or sprinklers, halogen or multiheaded lamps, extension cords, prohibited appliances, blocked doorways
Wall hangings
At least 24 inches from the ceiling; no more than half of a surface; not covering switches or outlets
General safety
Damage, excess trash, pests, unapproved pets, removed window screens, weapons

Step 8 of 13

Photos and confiscated items

Take a photo of anything you'll document in Roompact or an incident report.

Confiscated items, like candles, incense, space heaters, toasters, air fryers, hot plates, and waffle irons, get a strip of masking tape labeled with the room number and the resident's name if you know it.

Step 9 of 13

What we're not flagging, and microwaves

Don't flag curtains, refrigerators, or shot glasses.

Microwaves must have an internal smoke detector and automatic shutoff. If one doesn't, check the microwave box on the form and let the resident know if they're there. We're noting these, not taking them.

Unsure? Take a photo, note it, and James will decide.
