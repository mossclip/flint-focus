Step 7 of 12

Can't reach someone? The Attempted Contact Form

The Attempted Contact Form opens Monday, October 26. It's only for residents you truly couldn't connect with.

To use it, you need to document three attempts, using three different methods, at three different times. For example: a direct message, then an email, then a knock on their door, each on a different day. Two emails and a group chat message don't count.

Before you get there, try the simplest option: email the resident four or five 'Cuse Chat questions and ask them to write back. Once they reply and you log it, that chat is done.

Again, RD James knows that office hours have been a point of frustration for folks, so please bring your questions and concerns to your 1:1s.

Step 10 of 12

Health and Safety inspections: Wednesday, November 11

Flint's Health and Safety inspections are Wednesday, November 11. Every RA needs to be there, and nobody can take that night off. We need two RAs inspecting each floor.

We'll gather at 8:30 PM in our usual team meeting classroom to answer quick questions and get everyone on the same page. Then we'll head out to the floors. You won't inspect your own floor; assignments will be mixed so nobody has to write up their own residents.

Every room gets checked, whether the residents are home or not. You'll document what you find, with photos, and write it up. RD James will send full instructions in next week's Focus. For now, please get familiar with the prohibited items and fire safety expectations, since those are what we'll be looking for. Common ones:

Fire safety
Candles or incense (even unlit), evidence of smoking, covering or tampering with smoke detectors or sprinklers, tapestries or decorations on ceilings
Appliances
Halogen lamps, space heaters, rice cookers, toasters, crockpots, George Foreman-style grills
Other items
Weapons and firearms, electric scooters and e-bikes, SU property like signage, wall-mounted TVs, pets
The room itself
Damage or alterations to furniture, walls, or bathrooms
