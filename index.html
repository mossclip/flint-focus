Why The Flint Focus

Starting this week, announcements, updates, and dates live here instead of in our team meetings. A new Flint Focus goes up every Tuesday at 5:00 PM, and the password is in that day's email.

Here's why. I don't want our meetings to be me telling you to do this by that date. I'd rather we spend that time on things we do together: learning from each other, working at our tables, and having some fun.

Our meetings, our pace

How long a meeting takes is up to all of us. Each agenda has a pace built in. When we stay with it, we get through everything and get out sooner.

Several of you have told me the side conversations and running comments make meetings feel longer than they need to be. I hear that. This format is part of the fix. The rest is on all of us: respect each other's time and stay with the room.
